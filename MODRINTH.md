# Villager Milk Growth

Right-click a baby villager with a milk bucket in your main hand to advance its growth by five minutes. Four buckets grow a newborn into an adult.

Returns the empty bucket in survival. Includes green particles and a quiet drinking sound.

Requires Fabric Loader and Java 25. Install on the server, or locally for singleplayer; remote clients do not need it.

One JAR for Minecraft 26.1, 26.1.1, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3.
